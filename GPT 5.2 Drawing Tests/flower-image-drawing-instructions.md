### Step 1

**Goal:** Establish a consistent coordinate system on the 8.5" × 11" sheet.
**Action:** Place the paper in **portrait** orientation and add light measurement ticks so you can accurately place points by (x, y).
**Placement:**

* Define **origin O = (0.00, 0.00)** at the **bottom-left corner** of the page.
* x increases to the right (0 → 8.50).
* y increases upward (0 → 11.00).
* Along the **bottom edge**, make tiny ticks at x = 0, 1, 2, …, 8.
* Along the **left edge**, make tiny ticks at y = 0, 1, 2, …, 11.
  **Drawing Notes:** Use a very light neutral pencil (or the lightest pencil-crayon you have). Keep pressure extremely low; these are temporary reference marks.
  **Geometry Notes:** All marks in this step are **straight lines** (short ticks). Place each tick by measuring from O along the page edges.

### Step 7

**Goal:** Standardize the “loop unit” that will build the entire flower texture (so your loops are consistent and repeatable).
**Action:** Practice and then use this construction for every loop: a “leaf-shaped loop” anchored at C and aimed at a target point P.
**Placement:** For any chosen target point **P(xP, yP)** (one of the rim points or an interior point), construct the loop using these coordinate-based operations:

1. Identify the straight segment **CP**.
2. Mark a point **M** on CP at **55% of the distance from C to P** (M lies on the segment).
3. Through M, draw a tiny **perpendicular** tick (perpendicular to CP).
4. On that tick, mark two bulge points: **M₊** and **M₋**, each at the same distance from M on opposite sides.
5. Draw two smooth curves from C to P:

   * Curve A passes through M₊.
   * Curve B passes through M₋.
     These two curve lines meet at C and at P, forming a closed, airy “leaf loop.”
     **Drawing Notes:**

* Keep loops **thin, hairline, and light**, with a sharp pencil tip.
* Bulge distance guideline (choose by eye but keep consistent):

  * For long loops (C→P about 4"): bulge ≈ **0.50"–0.60"**.
  * For medium loops (~3"): bulge ≈ **0.35"–0.45"**.
  * For short loops (~2" or less): bulge ≈ **0.20"–0.30"**.
* Do not press hard; the final look relies on *many* light passes.
  **Geometry Notes:**
* The segment CP you reference is a **straight line** (guide only).
* The drawn loop itself is made of **two curve lines** (Curve A and Curve B) defined by passing through C → bulge point → P within the coordinate plane.
