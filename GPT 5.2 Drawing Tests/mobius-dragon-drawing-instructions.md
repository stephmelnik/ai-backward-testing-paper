Draw inside a portrait rectangle whose width is 100 units and height is 145 units (ratio 100:145).

Origin (0,0) is the bottom‑left corner of that rectangle.

x increases to the right, y increases upward.

Any point written as (x,y) means: measure x/100 of the rectangle’s width from the left edge, and y/145 of the rectangle’s height up from the bottom edge.
